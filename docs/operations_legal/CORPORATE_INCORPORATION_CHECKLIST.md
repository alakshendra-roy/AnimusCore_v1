## 1. OPC Structural Facts (Companies Act, 2013 + Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014, as amended 2021)

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Members** | Exactly **one** — a natural person who is an Indian citizen. The 2021 amendment also permits an NRI to be the sole member, with the residency threshold reduced from 182 to **120 days** in the preceding financial year — confirm current applicability with the CA if the Promoter's own residency status is a factor. |
| **Nominee** | Exactly **one** nominee must be named at incorporation (consent on **Form INC-3**), who becomes the member only if the original member dies or becomes incapacitated. The nominee can withdraw consent at any time; a replacement must be filed if they do. |
| **Directors** | Minimum **one** (can be the sole member), maximum **15** — an OPC is not restricted to a single director the way it is restricted to a single member. Decide up front whether this stays a sole-director OPC or appoints additional (non-member) directors for governance/banking purposes; §5 below assumes sole-director for the board-resolution mechanics. |
| **One OPC per person** | A person can be the sole member of **only one** OPC at a time, and cannot simultaneously be a nominee in more than one OPC either. |
| **Mandatory conversion threshold** | **Removed** by the Companies (Incorporation) Second Amendment Rules, 2021. There is no statutory paid-up-capital or turnover figure that *forces* conversion to Private Limited today — confirm this hasn't changed again before relying on it, but as of this document's drafting, no forced-conversion trigger applies. |
| **Voluntary conversion** | An OPC may convert to Private Limited voluntarily once it has completed **two years** from incorporation, with no financial threshold required to do so after that period. |
| **Restricted activities** | An OPC cannot be incorporated for, or convert into, a **Section 8 (non-profit)** company, and cannot carry on **non-banking financial investment activities** (including investment in securities of other body corporates). Neither restriction appears to bind Animus's actual business (software licensing, not investment activity) — flag to the CA for confirmation rather than assuming, since "investment activity" scoping has edge cases. |

## 4. MOA Object Clauses

Reuse `CORPORATE_SETUP_PACKAGE.md` Artifact 2's three object clauses, with one mechanical change: an OPC's MOA has a **single subscriber** (the sole member), so the subscriber block at the foot of the MOA lists one name, one signature, with the nominee's name and consent recorded separately (per Form INC-3) rather than as a co-subscriber. The object-clause language itself (what the Company is formed to do) does not change based on member count — do not re-draft it for this filing; only the subscription/execution block differs.

## 5. Board Resolution & Sole-Director Mechanics

`CORPORATE_SETUP_PACKAGE.md` Artifact 3 assumes **two** Directors present at a meeting — that template does not apply as-is to a sole-director OPC. Section 122(3)–(4) of the Companies Act, 2013 provides the OPC-specific mechanism instead: for an OPC with only one director, a resolution by that director, **entered in the minutes book and signed and dated by the director**, is deemed to have been passed at a duly convened Board meeting — no meeting notice, quorum, or second signature is required. If additional (non-member) directors are appointed later, ordinary Board-meeting mechanics (quorum, notice) apply from that point forward. A dedicated OPC sole-director resolution template (bank account opening + authorized-signatory designation, adapting `CORPORATE_SETUP_PACKAGE.md` Artifact 3's substantive resolved clauses to single-signature execution) should be drafted once the entity-type decision (see banner) is final — not duplicated here while that decision is still open.

## 6. Step-by-Step Filing Checklist (SPICe+, MCA V3 Portal, via IndiaFilings / LegalWiz or equivalent)

| Step | Action | Documents (§2) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Name reservation** — SPICe+ Part A, one or two proposed names | Promoter's PAN; business-activity description (§3) | Run your own MCA RUN availability search and an IP India trademark search before submitting, same reasoning as `CORPORATE_SETUP_PACKAGE.md` Artifact 1 §1.4. |
| **2** | **Class 3 DSC** for the sole director (+ any additional director) | PAN, Aadhaar, photo, live mobile/email for OTP, video KYC | Bundled by most platforms into the same flow. |
| **3** | **Nominee consent** — Form INC-3, signed by the nominee | Nominee's identity/address proof (§2) | Obtain this early — a filing cannot proceed to SPICe+ Part B without it, and it is the one step that structurally cannot be done by the promoter alone. |
| **4** | **Upload KYC + registered-office documents** | Full set per §2 | If using a platform-provided virtual office, use the platform's own NOC template, not the generic one referenced in §2. |
