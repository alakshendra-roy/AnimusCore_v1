> ## ⚠️ READ BEFORE FILING, SIGNING, OR SUBMITTING ANYTHING BELOW (original banner, historical)
> **Nothing in this document has been reviewed by a licensed Company Secretary (CS), Chartered Accountant (CA), advocate, or the receiving bank's own compliance/forex desk.** It is a founder-drafted starting point, formatted to be ready-to-adapt, not ready-to-file. Specifically:
>
> - **Name availability (Artifact 1)** has not been checked against MCA's live RUN database or the Trademark Registry. Both proposed names must be searched on the [MCA RUN portal](https://www.mca.gov.in) and [IP India's trademark search](https://ipindiaonline.gov.in) immediately before submission — a name that reads as available today may not be tomorrow, and this document cannot tell you the current answer.
> - **MOA main-object clauses (Artifact 2)** are the single highest-stakes item in this package: they define the company's legal capacity to act (an act outside the objects clause can be *ultra vires* and unenforceable). SPICe+ Part A runs proposed object language through an automated object-clause matching tool against MCA's own approved-object library — the drafting below is a starting point for that tool, per `LEGAL_INCORPORATION_BRIEF.md` §2 Step 3's own note, not guaranteed to pass it verbatim. Have a CS review and, if needed, re-word before filing.
> - **The board resolution (Artifact 3)** is a generic template. Most Indian banks (HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, and the fintech-partnered current-account products behind Razorpay Rize/IndiaFilings alike) require their **own specific board-resolution format** for current-account opening, often provided only after the account-opening application is initiated. Obtain and use the bank's own format if one exists; treat the draft below as a fallback only.
> - **RBI Purpose Code selection (Artifact 4)** is not a fact this document can settle. `P0802` and `P0807` both appear in the RBI purpose-code list for software-related receipts, and which one applies depends on how the specific transaction is characterized (standardized license fee vs. consultancy/implementation receipt — see `LEGAL_INCORPORATION_BRIEF.md` §5.2, which already flags this exact ambiguity). **The Authorized Dealer (AD) bank's forex/trade desk makes the final purpose-code determination at the time of e-FIRC issuance, based on the invoice and supporting documents you give them — not based on a code this document asserts.** Confirm before relying on the letter in Artifact 4.
> - This package assumes a **100% self-serve digital filing path** (Razorpay Rize / IndiaFilings or an equivalent platform), per your request — this is a different execution model from `LEGAL_INCORPORATION_BRIEF.md`, which was written as a handoff brief for an engaged CA/CS firm. The two are consistent on facts (names, NIC codes, object-clause themes, capital structure) but assume different *who does the filing*. Pick one path; don't run both simultaneously without reconciling who's actually submitting what, when.
> - **The second Director / nominee shareholder is still an open blocker** (`LEGAL_INCORPORATION_BRIEF.md` §3.1) — a Private Limited Company requires a minimum of two Directors and two Members under the Companies Act, 2013. Nothing in this package resolves that; it is the first thing to close before any step below has a real filing date.
> - **Resolved 2026-09-24:** the fork this bullet used to flag as open is now decided — [`CORPORATE_INCORPORATION_CHECKLIST.md`](CORPORATE_INCORPORATION_CHECKLIST.md)'s **One Person Company (OPC)** path is the official one, chosen specifically because it sidesteps the second-Director/Member blocker above by structural design (one member + one nominee, not two members). See the supersession banner at the top of this document.

*Refines and supersedes, for drafting purposes, the three object clauses in `LEGAL_INCORPORATION_BRIEF.md` §2 Step 3 — same underlying scope, re-sequenced to map explicitly onto low-latency systems development, IP licensing, telemetry SDK distribution, and algorithmic execution infrastructure. > ### III. OBJECTS OF THE COMPANY
>
> **(A) The objects to be pursued by the Company on its incorporation are:**
>
> **1. Development, Ownership, and Licensing of Low-Latency Systems Software and Intellectual Property.**
> To design, develop, test, own, maintain, and commercially license — on a proprietary basis, a dual-licensed (free evaluation and paid-enterprise) basis, or otherwise — computer software and systems software of every kind, including without limitation low-latency inter-process communication (IPC) engines, lock-free and shared-memory data-transport primitives, telemetry ingestion and event-processing systems, and any allied systems software; and to own, register, protect, assign, license, sublicense, or otherwise commercially exploit all intellectual property rights therein (including source code, object code, algorithms, benchmarking methodology, trademarks, and documentation), in India and abroad, whether pursuant to standardized license agreements, tiered enterprise entitlements, source-code escrow arrangements, or bespoke commercial agreements.
>
> **2. Software Development Kit (SDK) Distribution, Technical Support, and Systems Integration Services.**
> To develop, publish, distribute, license, and support software development kits (SDKs), application programming interfaces (APIs), and client libraries — including zero-dependency and zero-copy interoperability bridges between the Company's native systems software and third-party programming-language runtimes — enabling customers to integrate, consume, and process telemetry, market, and operational data produced or transported by the Company's software; and to provide technical support, consultancy, systems-integration, benchmarking, and custom engineering services in relation to such software and SDKs, to customers in India and abroad, whether under a standard support agreement, an enterprise service-level agreement, or a bespoke engineering engagement.
>
> **3. Algorithmic Execution Infrastructure and Cross-Border Software Export.**
> To design, develop, license, and support systems software infrastructure for algorithmic decision-making, automated order-execution, order-risk evaluation, and real-time signal-dispatch workloads, for deployment by customers in financial-trading, market-making, quantitative-research, and other latency-sensitive computing environments; and to export computer software, computer software licenses (including single-node, institutional-enterprise, OEM/redistribution, and global master-license structures), and associated technical support and maintenance services to persons resident outside India, in accordance with the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, and the rules, regulations, directions, and master directions made or issued thereunder from time to time.

## Artifact 4 — Forex & Inward Wire Documentation Templates

### 4.1 Cover Letter to Authorized Dealer Bank's Forex Desk — e-FIRC Request

## Artifact 5 — Step-by-Step Platform Execution Checklist (Digital Filing via Razorpay Rize / IndiaFilings or Equivalent)
