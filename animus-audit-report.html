Live Bug Diagnostic & Patch Verification — Zero-Copy Bridge

ItemDetail
Defectnanobind drain() returned a fresh copy every call, not a view — .ctypes.data alternated between 2 addresses across 49 calls.
Root causeDefault rv_policy::automatic copies whenever the ndarray has no owner and no self (nanobind nb_ndarray.cpp, source-verified).
PatchBound drain() with nb::rv_policy::reference_internal — attaches TelemetryStream as owner; 1-line binding change, no C++ function-body change.
VerificationCONFIRMED — pointer trace: 1 stable address / 49 calls (post-fix) vs. 2 alternating (pre-fix); lifetime-safety test (owner GC'd mid-view) passed; perf unchanged, 25.4 ns/event median.
